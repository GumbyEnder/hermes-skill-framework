
## Use this when

The user wants GEO guidance, AI answer visibility, brand mention tracking, citation analysis, or optimization for AI search surfaces.

## What good AI search visibility work should produce

- a clear sense of how the brand shows up in AI answers
- content structure that is easy for answer engines to parse
- entity consistency
- citation / mention opportunities
- gaps between classic SEO and AI visibility

## What to examine

- named entities
- authoritative source coverage
- content clarity
- structured data
- concise answer formats
- supporting evidence and citations

## Useful outputs

- visibility summary
- citation opportunities
- answer-engine content recommendations
- entity consistency checklist

## Human check

If the content is useful to a model but not to a reader, it is not finished.
