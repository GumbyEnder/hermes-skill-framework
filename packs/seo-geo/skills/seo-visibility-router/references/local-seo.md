The user wants help with map pack rankings, location pages, reviews, citations, or service-area visibility.

- location intent understanding
- business profile improvement ideas
- citation consistency checks
- page recommendations for local landing pages
- review and reputation notes

- business profile completeness
- NAP consistency
- location pages
- service-area coverage
- reviews and ratings
- local competitors

## Useful outputs

- local visibility checklist
- citation cleanup list
- location page plan
- review response suggestions
