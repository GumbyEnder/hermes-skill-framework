
## Use this when

The user wants to know whether visibility moved up or down, and why.

## What good rank tracking should produce

- current position trends
- winners and losers
- keyword groups that moved together
- possible causes for movement
- what to watch next

## What to look for

- position changes over time
- device and location differences
- branded vs non-branded movement
- page-level cannibalization
- volatility around updates

## Useful outputs

- rank trend summary
- movement table
- page association notes
- next actions

## Human check

A rank move without a business interpretation is just a number.
